Behavioral Archetypes — ANIMA Classification System
TRION classifies every behavioral entity into one of 12 archetypes based on its vector position in 128-dimensional behavioral space. Archetype assignment is continuous, not binary — an entity's archetype evolves as its behavioral history deepens.

TRION monitors 15 conditions (F1–F15) continuously and displays them publicly. If any condition shows FAIL, the oracle has failed. That is the promise.

Component Breakdown
Scoring Methodology
Physical Entropy
Shannon entropy of on-chain behavioral patterns. High entropy = diverse healthy activity. Low = repetitive or manipulated.
Market Intelligence
Observer-corrected intent coherence. Measures the alignment between behavioral signals and predicted market-rational behavior.
Network Consensus
Validator diversity and quorum coherence. Measures how much independent validator agreement exists on this entity's behavior.
Annotation Quality
Quality and completeness of behavioral annotations. Measures human-validator consensus and on-chain governance activity.
ANIMA Score
Archetype-normalized identity match score. How strongly this entity's behavior matches its assigned behavioral archetype pattern.

Behavioral Hash Specification — 93-Byte Canonical Format
entity_id(32B) ‖ event_type(1B) ‖ magnitude(8B) ‖ context(8B) ‖ timestamp(8B) ‖ chain_id(4B) ‖ block_hash(32B)
Total: 93 bytes
Strand A (sense) = SHA3-256(payload ‖ 0x00)
Strand B (antisense) = SHA3-256(payload ‖ 0xFF) XOR NOT(Strand A)
Tamper check = Strand A XOR Strand B == complement(Strand A)
