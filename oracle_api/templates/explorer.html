What Is a Silence Signal?

A Silence Signal is emitted when the composite coherence score C(t) falls below the dynamic threshold Θ(t). It is not an absence of data — it is a positive statement: "behavioral truth cannot be asserted for this entity at this time."

Θ(t) = 0.55 + 0.37·σ_vol where σ_vol is market volatility. In periods of extreme volatility, the bar rises — meaning fewer assets qualify for VALUATION signals.

Silence is information. An entity that repeatedly emits Silence Signals may be manipulated, low-depth, or behaviorally incoherent across chains. Etherscan cannot tell you this. TRION does.

The Falsifiability Monitor is the most important page on this explorer. No oracle, no data provider, no analytics tool in the history of finance has ever published the exact conditions under which it has failed. TRION publishes 15 conditions (F1–F15), monitors them continuously, and displays them publicly. If any condition is FAIL, TRION has failed. That is the promise.
